Buying a Used Car Without Getting Burned

Used Vehicles · July 9, 2026

A clean-looking listing and a friendly seller tell you nothing about what's actually happening under the hood. The paperwork trail matters more than the walk-around inspection most buyers rely on.

A vehicle history report catches accidents and title issues, but it won't catch a botched DIY repair or deferred maintenance — that's what an independent pre-purchase inspection is for, and it's worth the cost even on a car that "seems fine."

Advertisement

Service records that show consistent maintenance intervals are worth more than a lower odometer reading — a well-maintained car with 90,000 miles often outlasts a neglected one with 60,000.

Walk away from any seller who won't allow an independent inspection before the sale. That single refusal is a stronger red flag than anything visible from the driver's seat.
