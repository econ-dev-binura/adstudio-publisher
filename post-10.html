The Protein Myth: How Much Do You Actually Need

Nutrition · July 3, 2026

Supplement marketing has pushed the "more protein, always" message so hard that most people now overshoot their actual requirement without realizing it costs them anything.

For someone who isn't strength training seriously, roughly 0.8g per kilogram of bodyweight covers it. Active lifters need more, but the ceiling is lower than the shaker-bottle culture suggests — past a point, extra protein just gets used for energy or stored.

Where protein actually matters most is distribution across the day, not the daily total. Getting 20-30g at each meal builds and maintains muscle better than a single 100g dinner load.

Whole food sources also bring along fiber, micronutrients, and satiety that isolated powders don't — worth remembering before defaulting to a shake over an actual meal.
