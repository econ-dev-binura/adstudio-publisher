Chores by Age: What Kids Can Actually Handle

Kids · July 10, 2026

Parents tend to either wait too long to assign responsibility or hand over tasks a child isn't developmentally ready for — both end in frustration on both sides, and neither builds the habit that was actually the point.

By age three, putting toys away and helping set the table are realistic. By six, kids can handle feeding pets and making their own bed, even if the corners aren't sharp.

The jump that trips up most families is around nine to twelve — kids that age can genuinely manage laundry and simple meal prep, but only if they've been walked through it once rather than just assigned it cold.

Tying chores to an allowance works for some households and backfires in others; the deciding factor isn't the money, it's whether the chore still gets done on the weeks the payment is forgotten.
