The Scholarship Deadlines Most Students Miss

Scholarships · July 8, 2026

Most students focus scholarship hunting around the same two or three months every school does, which means the most competitive deadlines are also the most crowded. The better-funded, lower-competition ones sit outside that window entirely.

Niche and local scholarships — tied to a specific major, hobby, employer, or community group — often close a full semester before the big national ones, and get a fraction of the applicants.

Advertisement

Renewal requirements are the other thing that gets missed — a scholarship awarded freshman year can quietly lapse sophomore year if a GPA or credit-hour condition isn't tracked, and by the time it's noticed the appeal window is closed.

Setting calendar reminders a month ahead of both the application deadline and any renewal checkpoint catches almost all of this before it becomes a problem.
